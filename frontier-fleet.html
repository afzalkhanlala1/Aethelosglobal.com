Frontier Fleet

The community for truckers. A feed for life on the road, one button for roadside help and a light fleet map, in one app that installs on any phone and keeps working on a weak signal.

Open the early build (opens in a new tab)Ask about Frontier Fleet
Sector
Trucking
Stage
In build
Built for
Drivers, owner-operators and small fleets

Who it's for

  • Drivers and creators

    Post road life, share shorts and get help when the truck won't move.

  • Owner-operators

    Switch between the community and your own truck in one account.

  • Small fleets

    See the trucks whose drivers chose to share, and hear when one of them asks for help.

  • Businesses that serve trucks

    Mechanics, tow, tire, fuel and truck-friendly food, found at the moment a driver needs them.

What's in it

Built means built and tested. Next is what's being built now, in this order.

  • Feed and road reportsBuilt

    Photos, text posts and road reports, with Following, Hot Today, Top Drivers and Road Life feeds.

  • ShortsBuilt

    Videos up to 60 seconds, with trim, crop, text, filters and royalty-free music.

  • MessagesBuilt

    Direct messages, message requests and push notifications, with report, mute and block everywhere.

  • Live mapNext

    Your own truck, plus other trucks only when their drivers choose to share.

  • I Need HelpNext

    One button for tow, tire, fuel, lockout, mechanic or food, with your location shared only with the help you pick.

  • Front camNext

    Let your fleet or family watch the road ahead. Front camera only, and sound only if you switch it on.

Where it stands

In build. Accounts, the feed, shorts and messages are built and tested. The live map, roadside help and front cam come next, followed by a soft launch with a small group of drivers.

Rules it doesn't bend

  • Nothing goes live, whether location, camera or microphone, without an explicit, time-limited yes.
  • Stop sharing stays on screen whenever anything is live.
  • Exact GPS is never public, and photo metadata is stripped.
  • Front camera only: no cabin camera and no AI scoring of drivers.
  • Free for individual truckers. It will earn from boosted posts, business listings and plans for fleets of two or more trucks.
