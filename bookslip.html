Bookslip

Book clubs with the authors in them. Start a club, bring the author into the conversation, swap books with other readers and keep talking about them between meetings.

Get early access
Sector
Books and reading
Stage
In design
Built for
Readers, book clubs and authors

Who it's for

  • Readers

    Find a club for the book you're reading, or start one.

  • Book clubs

    Run the club in one place, from choosing the book to the last discussion.

  • Authors

    Meet the clubs reading your books and join their conversations.

What it will do

  • Clubs with authors

    Readers start a club around a book, and the author can join in.

  • Book exchange

    Pass on the books you've finished and pick up the ones other readers are offering.

  • Talk about the books

    A conversation for every club and every book that carries on between meetings.

Where it stands

In design. If you run a book club, or you're an author who'd like to meet your readers, we'd like to hear from you.
