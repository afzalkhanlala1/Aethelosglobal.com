Privacy

What this site collects, and what it doesn't.

No tracking

AethelosGlobal.com doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. The fonts are served from this site, so opening a page doesn't contact anyone else.

Messages you send

When you use the contact form, your name, email address and message are delivered to us by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-to-email service. We use them only to reply, and we don't sell or share them. To have a message deleted, email hello@aethelosglobal.com.

The floor dial

The dial remembers the last floor you visited in your browser's session storage, so the needle can travel from there. That stays on your device and is cleared when you close the tab.

Our companies' products

Each company here has its own privacy policy for its product. This page covers AethelosGlobal.com only.
