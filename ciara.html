Ciara

Rides and food delivery, run by AI. Ciara moves people and meals around the city, and AI does most of the operating work, with people stepping in when it matters.

Get early access
Sector
Mobility and delivery
Stage
Rebuilding
Built for
Riders, diners, drivers and restaurants

Who it's for

  • Riders

    Name your fare, pick an offer and follow the ride.

  • Diners

    Order from local restaurants and track the delivery.

  • Drivers

    Choose the trips you want, with earnings and support in the app.

What it will do

  • Rides for every budget

    From motorbikes to premium cars, plus city-to-city trips and couriers.

  • You name the fare

    Riders propose a price and nearby drivers send offers.

  • Food delivery

    Meals from local restaurants, in the same app as your rides.

  • Support that sorts itself out

    An AI agent reads the booking, resolves most problems on the spot and hands bigger disputes to a person.

  • Report it out loud

    Describe a problem by voice and Ciara drafts the support ticket.

  • Safety on every trip

    Live tracking, an emergency alert and incident reports for riders and drivers.

Where it stands

Rebuilding. The first version handled rides, couriers and AI support. The new one adds food delivery and hands more of the day-to-day work to AI.

Where the line is

  • When the AI can't settle a problem after a few tries, or the amount in dispute is large, a person takes over.
