Virtual Tour Studio

360° walkthroughs, captured on a phone. Follow the floating targets to shoot a room, mark its doors, walk through them one by one, then publish the whole place as a tour anyone can click through or embed on their own site.

Ask for a demo
Sector
Property and places
Stage
Field trials
Built for
People who show places online, and the people who shoot tours for them

Who it's for

  • Tour creators

    Shoot, stitch, link and publish a whole property from a phone, then hand the client a link or an embed.

  • Places worth a look

    Property, hospitality and venues: anywhere visitors want to look around before they arrive.

What it does

  • Guided capture

    Hold each orb in the centre circle and the phone takes the shot when it's steady. Every frame is saved on the phone as it's taken, so a dropped connection loses nothing.

  • The whole sphere

    The capture plan adapts to the lens and includes the ceiling and floor. A coverage report shows anything it missed.

  • Doors and branches

    Mark every exit, explore them one at a time, loop back to rooms you've already shot, and mark dead ends as finished.

  • Publish and embed

    A stable public link and a copy-and-paste embed. Draft edits stay private until you republish.

  • Bring your own photos

    Import overlapping photos or finished 360° panoramas, and export any tour as a static site.

How it works

  1. Capture the first room

    Stand in one spot, turn around the lens and let the targets guide you.

  2. Check the sphere

    Review the ceiling and floor, and add a sweep if anything is missing.

  3. Mark the exits

    Name each door, corridor or stair you want to explore.

  4. Walk to the next room

    Pick an exit, walk through it and capture where you land.

  5. Publish

    Choose the opening view, then share the link or the embed.

Where it stands

Field trials. Capture, editing, publishing and embedding are built and pass automated and browser checks. Trials on real phones come next.

Where the line is

  • The connection diagram shows how rooms link, not measured distances or a floor plan.
  • Finishing the targets isn't proof of seamless coverage on every phone, which is why it shows you what it missed before you publish.
