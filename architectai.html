ArchitectAI

Concept design for residential studios. Pick a real parcel, enter the site controls and describe the house in plain language. ArchitectAI returns dimensioned plans, up to four alternates, a coordinated A3 concept set and a walkable 3D model.

Sector
Architecture
Stage
Available
Built for
Residential architecture studios

Who it's for

  • Residential studios

    Run a client's brief before the first meeting and walk in with options and a site plan.

  • Feasibility work

    Test what fits on a lot under its setbacks, coverage and height before anyone draws by hand.

What it does

  • Parcel to plot

    Click beside a road and it proposes a plot boundary from mapped road geometry. Adjust the frontage and depth, or drag any corner.

  • Rules draft it

    A solver builds complete room programs across one to three stories inside your setbacks, coverage and height, and explains when a brief can't be built.

  • Up to four alternates

    Distinct valid layouts with area and circulation totals, editable in 2D with undo.

  • A coordinated set

    A3 concept sheets, layered DXF, SVG, PNG and model JSON from one saved model, so a moved wall moves everywhere.

  • Walk through it

    A furnished 3D model with preliminary water and electrical routes, floor by floor.

How it works

  1. Pick the plot

    Choose a real parcel and adjust its boundary.

  2. Set the controls

    Enter setbacks, coverage and height, then describe the house.

  3. Rules draft it

    The solver lays out every room inside your limits.

  4. Compare and edit

    Review up to four alternates and edit in 2D.

  5. Export and walk through

    Take the A3 set, the DXF and the 3D model to your client.

Where it stands

Available. Studios can book a 20-minute walkthrough and put a real brief through it.

Where the line is

  • ArchitectAI does concept design. Structural design, detailed MEP engineering, local amendments and permit release stay with the studio, and every sheet is marked concept, not for construction.
  • Rules decide and AI advises. The language model reads the brief and suggests edits, but it never draws geometry and can't quietly drop a room you asked for.
