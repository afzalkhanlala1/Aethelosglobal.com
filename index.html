How we build

The eight companies serve very different people. They share four rules.

  • Show the working

    A figure nobody can check is a figure nobody should use. Quanta links every amount to the rule and the evidence behind it. ArchitectAI draws with rules, so every dimension can be traced. Decision OS says why each signal fired.

    Seen inQuantaArchitectAIDecision OS

  • People make the call

    The software does the legwork and a person makes the decision. Care Companion's AI never diagnoses; a licensed doctor reviews every case. In Quanta, a person chooses the injury category, never the model. Ciara's AI support hands bigger disputes to a person.

    Seen inCare CompanionQuantaCiara

  • Say what it can't do

    Every ArchitectAI sheet is marked concept, not for construction. Virtual Tour Studio shows you the parts of a room it missed. Quanta says plainly that it isn't ready for live client work until practitioners and real outcomes have tested it.

    Seen inArchitectAIVirtual Tour StudioQuanta

  • Nothing shared without asking

    Frontier Fleet never turns on location, camera or microphone without a time-limited yes from the driver, and Stop sharing is always on screen. Care Companion shares a patient's care with family only when the patient allows it.

    Seen inFrontier FleetCare Companion
